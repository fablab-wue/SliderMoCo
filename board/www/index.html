  • Tap MOVE (≤ ⅓ s): cruise stays on. Hold longer: runs while held. Same direction or ⏹ stops cruise.
  • ⏹ tap: stop. Hold 1 s: halt. Hold 2 s: disable.
  • SPEED is commanded SS. Footer Spd is actual.
  • Window = soft limits. AB = marks. Timelapse survives phone sleep. Timeline Play is desktop only and does not.
